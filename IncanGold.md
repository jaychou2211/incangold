# <mark>Incan Gold</mark> Game Rules

## 1. Game Introduction
Incan Gold is a push-your-luck game designed by Alan R. Moon and Bruno Faidutti.
Explorers take on the roles of <mark>explorers</mark> venturing deep into an ancient Incan temple in search of treasures, constantly making decisions between continuing to explore for more wealth or safely retreating.

---

## 2. Game Elements
- Exploration phases: The game consists of 5 exploration <mark>rounds</mark>.
- Exploration elements:
  - <mark>Treasure</mark>: Values range from 1 to 17, when discovered, <mark>points</mark> are evenly distributed among all explorers still on the <mark>adventure</mark>, with any remaining points left in the <mark>corridor</mark>.
  - <mark>Artifact</mark>: Special treasures with values of 5, 7, 8, 10, and 12 ( points ).
  - <mark>Hazard</mark>: Five types ( <mark>Spider</mark>, <mark>Snake</mark>, <mark>Flame</mark>, <mark>Rockslide</mark>, <mark>Mummy</mark> ).
  When the same type of <mark>hazard</mark> appears twice, the current <mark>round</mark> immediately ends, and all explorers still on the <mark>adventure</mark> lose all their points collected in this round (imagine explorers fleeing in panic and having to leave behind their heavy treasures).
- Explorer choices: In each <mark>turn</mark> of a <mark>round</mark>, explorers must decide whether to continue the <mark>adventure</mark> or <mark>retreat</mark>.
- Scoring: explorers secure points by retreating to their <mark>camp</mark>. 
The explorer with the highest total score at the end of the game wins.

---

## 3. Game Flow
**Game setup**: 
The system randomly generates an exploration  <mark>deck</mark>, and explorers prepare to begin their adventure.

**Each turn within a round proceeds as follows**:
- System reveals one card.
  - artifact -> the artifact remains in the corridor.
  - treasure -> the points is evenly distributed among the explorers in the corridor.
  - hazard -> and it's the second appearance of the same type in this round, the round ends immediately.

**Each explorer chooses whether to continue exploring or retreat.**

**Round ending conditions**:
- All explorers choose to retreat.
- A second hazard card of the same type appears.

**Scoring**:
- Explorers who actively retreat:
  - Can evenly split the points and artifacts left in the corridor (remaining undivided points and artifact stay in the corridor).
  - Note: An artifact can only be taken when exactly one explorer retreats.
  - Safely store all acquired points and artifacts in their camp.
- Explorers still in the corridor when a hazard ends the round lose all points collected in that round.
- At the end of each round, all points and artifacts remaining in the corridor are cleared.

**Game end**: After completing 5 rounds, total points are calculated, and the explorer with the highest score wins.
